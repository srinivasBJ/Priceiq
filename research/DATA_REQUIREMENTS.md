# Data requirements for publication-quality evaluation

The current uploaded data are insufficient for the primary historical price-timing experiment.

A suitable input must have a stable product identifier; repeated timestamped marketplace prices for the same product; numeric, currency-consistent positive prices; source/provenance; at least 21 observed dates and 21 observations per product under the current quality gate; and enough observations after valid decision points to observe the seven-day horizon. It must distinguish observation timestamps from transaction timestamps and must not be interpolated or converted from a catalog snapshot.

The original production PriceIQ deterministic algorithm and catalog are confirmed lost and will not be recovered or recreated. A Full-legacy-PriceIQ result remains unavailable. PriceIQ Research v1.0 can instead be calibrated only after a genuine historical input is supplied, and must report itself as a new methodology.
